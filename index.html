Athletic Nutrition Guidelines

Protein Allocation (Repair)

For active individuals and athletes, aim for 0.8–1.0 grams of protein per pound of lean body weight daily. The High Protein split profile is ideal for lean muscle preservation and enhanced structural recovery.

Hydration Thresholds (Performance)

Dehydration of just 2% of body weight can reduce metabolic efficiency and cognitive focus. Set your target to at least half your body weight in ounces of water per day, plus 12-16 ounces for each hour of strenuous training.

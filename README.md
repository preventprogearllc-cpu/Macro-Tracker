# Interactive Weekly Calorie Tracker Dashboard This single-file web application serves as a robust personal calorie tracker [1]. It has been designed with a premium, clean, athletic-inspired slate aesthetic to match high-performance training and recovery workflows. ### Key Features Implemented: 1. **7-Day Rolling Storage:** Automatically saves your daily calorie logs using the browser's `localStorage` so your data persists when you close or refresh the page [1]. 2. **Weekly Overview Panel:** Displays your total weekly calories consumed, your combined weekly limit (7 days of your daily goal), and your remaining or excess budget. 3. **Interactive History Cards:** Click on any day in the weekly view to look back, edit, or back-fill logs for that specific day. 4. **Interactive Indicators:** Displays green or red color-coded progress bars to easily signal whether you are under or over your daily and weekly limits. 5. **Integrated BMR & Activity Target Calculator:** Added a dynamic calculator to settings using the Mifflin-St Jeor formula. It provides caloric recommendations based on five distinct activity levels and lets you apply any level as your daily target with a single click. 6. **Pre-Populated Mock Data:** Automatically populates with a few days of sample athletic and recovery meals on your first load so you can immediately see how the weekly analytics look.

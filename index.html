``` --- ## Technical Details & Operations Guide ### 1. Storing Weekly Data (`localStorage`) The application operates entirely on your front-end local instance [1]. It serializes your target goals and daily itemized logs into a stringified JSON object [1]. Every time you: * Add or remove a food item, * Adjust your daily calorie goals, * Apply a calculation from the BMR settings tool, The app writes directly to `localStorage` under the identifier `calorieTrackerData` [1]. This guarantees that even if you close your browser or restart, your logs remain safe. ### 2. BMR & TDEE Calculations (Mifflin-St Jeor) To display highly tailored activity level recommendations, we have implemented the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. Here is how your daily target can be computed: * **Metric Conversion:** Weight in pounds is multiplied by $0.45359237$ to get kilograms. Height in inches is multiplied by $2.54$ to get centimeters. * **Base BMR Formulation:** * **Men:** $BMR = (10 \times \text{Weight}_{\text{kg}}) + (6.25 \times \text{Height}_{\text{cm}}) - (5 \times \text{Age}_{\text{years}}) + 5$ * **Women:** $BMR = (10 \times \text{Weight}_{\text{kg}}) + (6.25 \times \text{Height}_{\text{cm}}) - (5 \times \text{Age}_{\text{years}}) - 161$ * **Activity-Based Recommendations (TDEE):** * **Sedentary:** $BMR \times 1.2$ * **Lightly Active:** $BMR \times 1.375$ * **Moderately Active:** $BMR \times 1.55$ * **Very Active:** $BMR \times 1.725$ * **Extra Active:** $BMR \times 1.9$ Clicking **"Apply"** next to any of these outputs immediately saves it as your active daily target, which dynamically shifts your weekly progress thresholds instantly.
